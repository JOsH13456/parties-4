NZ Future Forward Party is fighting for reliable public transport and real climate action — because every generation deserves a country that works for them.

Our manifesto

Vision & values

NZ Future Forward Party exists because the decisions made today shape the country the next generation inherits. We believe every New Zealander — no matter their postcode, income, or age — deserves reliable transport, a healthy environment, and a fair shot at the future. We are guided by three core values: fairness, so that the cost of getting around and the cost of climate change don't fall hardest on those who can least afford it; sustainability, so that our roads, rivers, and native wildlife are protected rather than sacrificed for short-term convenience; and accountability, so that the policies we promise are the policies we deliver. We don't believe in choosing between a thriving economy and a thriving environment. We believe in building both — cleaner transport, stronger communities, and a country that's still worth living in fifty years from now.

Our policy areas

Transport System Reworked!!

Overview

This is important for politicians to address as they should want the people of NZ to be free of stress while using the transport system, citizens should be concerned as many people from their own country or even themselves are being affected by the poor public transport systems in place at the moment.

Why it matters

There is more than one specific problem; like how public transport is too expensive, traffic congestion, rural areas not having enough access to public transport. The poor reliability of the system has caused all these problems. More than 30% of all NZ students struggle with the expenses and unreliability of the buses, trains, or ferries.

Current policy

The Policy Statement on Land Transport is one current policy for this matter, there are many issues which include traffic congestion still being a major problem in big cities, rural areas still not having enough access to public transport, the system still being very unreliable with many delays, and many people still using private cars which increases emissions.

How we'll do it

What will our policies be on this matter? How does it solve the problem better than the current policy? What are the benefits? Any obstacles?

  • Reduce fares for students, young people and elders (over 65) by 50%
  • Increase the number of each transportation method
  • Improve transport in rural areas
  • Increase the amount of electric buses
  • Increase the number of cycling / walking paths

Our main policies on this matter will be: reducing fares for students, young people and elders (over 65) by 50%, increasing the number of each transportation method, improving transport in rural areas, increasing the amount of electric buses, and increasing the number of cycling/walking paths. These policies solve the problem of public transport being too expensive for low income families, traffic congestion, the amount of carbon emissions, and safer transport in rural communities. Some benefits can be cleaning up the environment by using electric buses, more frequent users of public transport which can also affect the number of people using private cars. There are also some obstacles that we would face while working on these changes such as the time it will take for some of these major projects, the long term investment needed, and the patience from the citizens of NZ.

Summary

Our first policy will be about transport, we will reduce fares by 50% for students, young people and people over 65 years, increase the number of each method of transportation, increase the number of electric buses, and increase the number of methods of transport in rural communities. These will help with many things such as, helping low income families, reducing the number of carbon emissions, providing a safer commute to work/school, and reduced traffic congestion. These will help NZ get one step closer to being a perfect thriving nation.

Moving to the Future Greener and Cleaner!

Overview

This issue on our climate/environment is important for politicians to address as they should care for our country and environment and how it can affect people, native species and communities all around NZ.

Why it matters

There is more than one specific problem but they all revolve around our environment that we live in: climate change causes more floods and storms around some areas on NZ such as Whenuapai, native plants/animals are losing their habitats, also the whole of NZ needing more renewable energy sources for the future, pollution has also taken over many rivers all around NZ.

Current policy

The Emission Reduction Plan is one policy that has been put in place for this matter, there are still many problems such as; NZ still producing a lot of greenhouse gases, native plants and animals still endangered/under threat, pollution still affecting some rivers, lakes, and beaches, which can also endanger species on land and marine life.

How we'll do it

What will our policies be on this matter? How does it solve the problem better than the current policy? What are the benefits? Any obstacles?

  • Plant over 10 million native trees / plants
  • Invest in more renewable energy sources for the future
  • Protect wildlife by placing pest control traps
  • Reduce carbon emissions

Our policies that we will implement on these matters will be: planting over 10 million native trees/plants, investing in more renewable energy sources for the future, protecting wildlife by placing pest control traps, and reducing carbon emissions. These policies that we have chosen will help weaken the damages of climate change and causing less natural disasters, pollution harming rivers and lakes, and native plants/animals losing their habitat. Some benefits could be: cleaner beaches for the public, a cleaner future by renewable energy sources, more native animals/plants thriving. There are also some obstacles such as getting enough citizens who are willing to volunteer to plant the native trees, getting funding for these long term renewable energy projects, and funding for enough pest control traps for all around NZ.

Summary

Our second policy will be about the environment and sustaining our wildlife for the future, we will plant over 10 million native trees and plants, invest in more renewable energy sources for the future, protect wildlife by planting more pest control traps, and reduce carbon emissions with electric buses which can also link to our transport policies. This will build a strong foundation for NZ's future.
